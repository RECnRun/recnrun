RECnRun

Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

RECnRun is a browser extension that records and replays browser sessions, and the website at recnrun.com. This policy covers both.

In short

Your recordings stay in your browser. RECnRun has no servers that receive them, sells no data, and runs no analytics, ads, or tracking.

What the extension stores

The extension keeps the recordings you make in its own storage in your browser (chrome.storage.local), on your device. A recording holds what you captured in that session: the address of the page where it started, when it started, how long it lasted, and its events, such as clicks, typed text, navigation, and changes to the page. Typed text can include personal or sensitive information; it stays on your device with the rest of the recording.

When you open the dashboard from a page, the extension reads that tab's address to show the recordings for that site. It does not store or send that address.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The extension does not send your recordings or any other data to RECnRun or to anyone else. On recnrun.com, the extension shows its dashboard over the site in a frame; the website cannot read it, and the extension tells the website only its own version number.

The website

recnrun.com sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Keeping and deleting your data

Recordings stay until you delete them. Removing the extension from your browser deletes everything it stored.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

RECnRun's use of the information it handles complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

We publish changes to this policy on this page and update its effective date.
