# E8: Routes A and B in Chrome without any CDP client

Spec: `tests/experiments/E8-strict.spec.ts`, run by `vp run test:experiments`. Result: Google Chrome 156.0.8078.79 on Linux in Docker, load method `managed-policy`, 2026-11-03, condition `strict`, run `2026-11-03T18-43-45.998Z-4ddb78fe-6c33-4b11-8406-d5ecfb3cecef` (9.1 min, full sample sizes), after Route A's fix of the held restart. This report is written by hand from that run's `verdicts.json` and `samples.json`; the first run, `2026-11-03T17-54-48.044Z-7b20b7b0-848c-4f90-8e2b-c34b44367d56` (12.5 min), is kept below where it differs. Nothing here is validated on macOS or Windows yet.

Every other experiment runs Chrome with `--remote-debugging-port=0` and a Playwright `connectOverCDP` session attached to the browser, which E4 and E6 name as a possible confound: that session auto-attaches to workers and resumes the ones waiting for a debugger. E8 repeats the hypotheses of E3 and E6 that the confound could change, with the isolator as the only debugger client.

## Hypotheses and verdicts

A sample of `a-cold-start` passes when Route A held the cold service worker at its instrumentation pause (`pause.paused` in the journal), the worker's first request (`/me?from=/sw-main.js?variant=classic`) carried `__rec=<marker>`, the worker reported `__rec:<marker>:fixture-sw`, and the origin's IndexedDB and Cache Storage hold the marked `fixture-sw` and no unprefixed one. A `b-restart` sample passes when the page loads again after the lab stopped its worker with `Target.closeTarget`, and the restarted worker's first request and its database carry the tab's marker (until the run below fixed the held restart, the sample passed on any load, marked or not). A `c-selection-workers` sample passes as E6's `c-workers` does: after a switch from slot 6 to slot 7, the workers open `__rec:slot-7:fixture-<realm>`. `d-other-clients` passes when `chrome.debugger.getTargets()` lists no attached target when the isolator's lab starts, before it arms anything.

## How it runs

`tests/browser/fixtures/strict-lab.ts` writes `/etc/opt/chrome/policies/managed/recnrun.json`: `ExtensionSettings` force-installs the isolator's CRX from a loopback update server, `DefaultNotificationsSetting: 2` denies the fixture's push prompt (the CDP runs deny it with `Browser.setPermission`), and `3rdparty.extensions.<id>.lab` carries the lab's value, which the isolator reads through `chrome.storage.managed` (`apps/isolator/src/background/lab`, schema `public/lab-schema.json`). Chrome starts through `launchChrome({ cdp: false })`: no `--remote-debugging-*` flag, so no CDP client can exist. The isolator posts a `loaded` beacon (with the targets it saw at start) and then its journal to `http://lab.localhost:<port>/lab` on the same loopback server; the harness only reads those posts and the fixture's ledger.

The spec drives Chrome only with the keyboard on the X display: `ctrl+t` opens a tab, the omnibox loads `offline.html` (a static page of the origin), where the lab arms the tab before any script of the app runs and clears the origin with `Storage.clearDataForOrigin` on the arm's own session, and then the page under test (`sw.html?sw=classic` or `workers.html?type=classic`). On each later load of an armed tab the lab reads, in the page's ISOLATED world, the fixture's `#report` and `indexedDB.databases()`, and, on the tab session, `CacheStorage.requestCacheNames`; `IndexedDB.requestDatabaseNames` answers `-32601` over `chrome.debugger`. Commands go through the policy: `stop` closes every worker target of the origin with Route A's `closeTarget` (the harness repeats it until the journal lists none), and `selection` points the session rules at a slot. `ctrl+w` closes the tab after each sample, which ends its shared worker. `b-restart` reloads the same armed tab, and records `held` when no report arrives within 10 s, then presses Escape. Route B arms each tab with `urlPattern` `*worker.js?type=*` and the `sync-xhr` selection. The pure evaluation lives in `tests/experiments/e8-samples.ts`, tested by `tests/e8-samples.test.ts`; the steps are in `tests/experiments/e8-loads.ts` and `tests/browser/fixtures/strict-steps.ts`.

## Observations

- **No other client exists.** The lab's first `getTargets()` listed 8 targets, none attached: two extension service workers, an extension background page, a `page` and an `other` at `about:blank`, an `other` with no URL, and the omnibox popup's two WebUI targets.
- **Route A holds a cold worker without any CDP client.** In 20 of 20 samples the worker paused at its instrumentation breakpoint, its single first request carried the tab's marker, it reported `__rec:<marker>:fixture-sw`, and the origin held only `__rec:<marker>:fixture-sw` in IndexedDB and `__rec:<marker>:fixture-sw` and `__rec:<marker>:fixture-shell-v1` in Cache Storage. This matches E3's `before-script-execution` (120 of 120 with the CDP session).
- **A worker the isolator stopped restarts marked.** In run `2026-11-03T18-43-45.998Z-4ddb78fe-6c33-4b11-8406-d5ecfb3cecef`, after the lab's `closeTarget` in the armed tab, the reload loaded in 10 of 10 samples, and the restarted worker's first request carried `__rec=<marker>` and it reported `__rec:<marker>:fixture-sw`. Each sample's `trace` shows the restart as a second `Target.attachedToTarget`, `waitingForDebugger: true`, under a new target id, after `closeTarget` had detached the tab's child session on the old one (`Target.detachFromTarget` answered `ok` 19 times, recorded as `debugger.worker-child-session-detach` in the run's `capabilities.json`; the other closes were of a second worker of the origin). In the first run, before the fix, the reload never loaded within 10 s in 10 of 10 samples (`held` 10, then a sample passed on any load, marked or not); `tests/browser/strict-devtools.spec.ts` showed the same hold after DevTools closed and now shows the marked restart. With the CDP session, E3's `cold-start-navigation` restarts in a new tab still load unmarked (`attached while running, not waiting for the debugger` in 100 samples).
- **Why the restart is held (diagnosis run `2026-11-03T18-18-39.449Z-023bc3c1-e497-429e-9e82-e6c080cae0ce`, `b-restart` only, `held` 10 of 10).** Route A's journal now keeps every `Target.attachedToTarget`, `Target.detachedFromTarget`, and `Target.targetInfoChanged` on the armed tab's sessions, every `onDetach`, and the times of `closeTarget`'s steps; each `b-restart` sample keeps that slice as `trace`. In 10 of 10 samples the service worker was auto-attached once, at its registration (`waitingForDebugger: true`); `closeTarget` answered `ok` on the worker's own session and its direct session detached `ok` within 1 to 3 ms; no `Target.detachedFromTarget` ever came for the worker's auto-attached child session; and after the reload no `Target.attachedToTarget` came at all, and no `Target.targetInfoChanged`. So the tab's child session on the worker survives the stop, and Route A, which bootstraps and releases only on `attachedToTarget`, never sees the restart. The `Target.detachedFromTarget` events that did arrive (9) were for another worker of the origin, auto-attached when the next tab was armed, whose registration that arm's `Storage.clearDataForOrigin` removed. Inferred from Chromium, not observed: `ServiceWorkerDevToolsManager` keeps a stopped worker's agent host with its sessions and starts the worker again with `pause_on_start` set when a session is still attached, so the restart waits for that stale session's `Runtime.runIfWaitingForDebugger`. That would also explain why, once the session is detached, the restart gets a new target id: no session keeps the stopped host. The earlier inference that the target id persists across the restart is refuted on the fixed path and was never observed on the held one. In the CDP runs Playwright's own session resumed it, and E3 restarted the worker in a new tab, whose auto-attach had no stale session. Route A's `closeTarget` now detaches that child session once the close answers `ok`.
- **The slot switch reaches workers without any CDP client.** Every second load opened `__rec:slot-7:fixture-dedicated` and `__rec:slot-7:fixture-shared` after a first load under slot 6, as in E6's `c-workers` (100 of 100). `updateSessionRules` took 0.3 to 1.6 ms. The workers' own `/me` still failed (`TypeError: Failed to fetch` or `Error: no report within 10 s`), as E6 attributes to E5's Local Network Access block.

## Not covered

Module, imported, static, and dynamic service-worker variants (only the classic one), dedicated workers under Route A, `direct-attach-race`, `pre-existing-running`, a restart in a new tab as E3 did it, Route C, the popup and dashboard (this build has none), egress checks beyond the isolator's posts to `lab.localhost`, the stress conditions (`a-cold-start` is tagged `@order-critical`, but no stress run took it), and Chrome outside Linux or installed other than by policy.

## Reproducing

`vp run test:experiments -- tests/experiments/E8-strict.spec.ts` appends the four verdicts to `.artifacts/evidence/<run>/verdicts.json` and writes each test's `samples.json` under `test-results/experiments/<test>/` (inside `.artifacts/docker/<run>/` for a Docker run).
