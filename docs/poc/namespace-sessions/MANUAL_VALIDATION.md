# Manual validation

Everything in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) that needs a person: the branded Chrome on a desktop with its own profile, Chrome's dialogs and infobars, DevTools, a real third-party application, or the strict form of T23 where no runner and no observer exist. Each item runs after the automated gate of its phase and before the report is final (P8.2). Record the date, machine, OS, Chrome version, extension build hash, and result under the item; a failed item opens a regression spec (`tests/browser/regressions/<id>.spec.ts`) before anything else.

Why these are manual: Chrome 137 removed `--load-extension` from branded builds, so loading an unpacked extension into the person's own Chrome is a `chrome://extensions` gesture; the `debugger` infobar and the install warning are browser UI the harness hides or never shows; DevTools coexistence needs a person to open DevTools; a real Keycloak/Angular/Dexie application or a public PWA needs network egress the tests forbid; and T23 in its strict form forbids the very CDP connection a test runner uses.

Not exercised yet: closing the infobar or pressing Cancel, and what the diagnostics report after that.
  Also observed on this run, by hand in the same profile: Route C `install({ marker: "slot-0" })`, `setMarker({ marker: "slot-1" })`, `setOriginal()` moved `storage.html` between `__rec:slot-0:fixture-page`, `__rec:slot-1:fixture-page`, and `fixture-page`, and refused `new Worker` and `new SharedWorker` (E7); under the original, `indexedDB.databases()` listed no `__rec:*` name and `indexedDB.open("__rec:x:y")` threw `SecurityError` (P1.7). ## After P1

- **M-P1.a DevTools on a managed target.** With the extension attached to a fixture tab and its service worker, open DevTools on the tab and on the worker (`chrome://serviceworker-internals` → Inspect). Record what the extension's `chrome.debugger.onDetach` reports (`replaced_with_devtools` or otherwise), whether the bootstrap still won on the next worker start, and whether the extension recovered when DevTools closed.
  Lab result (container, CDP present): 2026-11-03, Google Chrome 156.0.8078.79 on Linux in Docker (Xvfb), [`tests/browser/devtools-coexistence.spec.ts`](../../../tests/browser/devtools-coexistence.spec.ts), Playwright's own CDP session attached to every target throughout. Tab: F12 opened DevTools docked (`devtools_app.html`); `chrome.debugger.onDetach` reported nothing (no `replaced_with_devtools`) and the extension's tab session stayed. With DevTools open, and again after F12 closed it, a worker restarted by navigation came back unmarked (`fixture-sw`, E3: such a worker is never held) and a new registration after `clearOrigin` came back marked. F12 right after a reload sometimes reached no view (about one run in three), so the spec presses it again. Worker: Inspect at `chrome://serviceworker-internals` opened an undocked `DevTools` window (`worker_app.html`); no detach was reported and the tab's auto-attach still marked the next new registration. The window outlived its worker's registration and closed on ctrl+w, with the same results after. Nothing detached in either case, so recovery on close was not exercised.
  Strict lab result (container, no CDP client): 2026-11-03, same Chrome and spec as M-P0.a's strict line. DevTools docked on the armed tab (F12) and DevTools on the worker (`chrome://serviceworker-internals`, Inspect, a window titled `DevTools`) both left the isolator's tab session attached: no `onDetach` (`tabDetached` stayed unset), and `chrome.debugger.getTargets()` listed them as `devtools_app.html` and `worker_app.html`. While DevTools was open, the worker the isolator stopped restarted unmarked (`fixture-sw`), resumed by DevTools, and a new registration after clearing the origin was still marked. Once DevTools closed, the isolator alone left the restarted worker waiting on a debugger and the reload never loaded ([E8](experiments/E8.md) `b-restart`, 10 of 10). Since Route A's `closeTarget` detaches the tab's child session on the stopped worker (run `2026-11-03T18-36-02.527Z-90926156-3be2-4293-bd02-ef36edd8e18e`), the restart after DevTools closes, on the tab and on the worker, comes back marked (`__rec:<marker>:fixture-sw`), and E8 `b-restart` passes 10 of 10; while DevTools is open the restart is still unmarked.
